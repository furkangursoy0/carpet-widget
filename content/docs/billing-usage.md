# Billing & usage

## How room previews are counted

You're billed **per successful generation** — when a shopper uploads a photo and Sceneva successfully composes their room with your rug.

You're **not** billed for:
- Widget impressions (just showing the button)
- Clicks to open the modal
- Failed generations (we eat the cost)
- Downloads or shares of an already-generated preview

## Growth plan — $99/month

- 1,000 room previews per month
- Resets on your monthly billing date
- Overage: $0.08 per preview (only kicks in if you exceed your monthly allotment)

## Billing dates & invoices

Invoices are issued by **Lemon Squeezy** (our merchant of record). They handle EU VAT, US sales tax, and other regional taxes automatically.

Manage subscription, payment method, and invoices from **Billing → Manage subscription**.

## Cancelling

Cancel anytime from the billing portal. You'll keep access until the end of the current billing period.
