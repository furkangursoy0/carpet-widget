# Why we built Sceneva

Anyone who's ever bought a rug online knows the moment: you find one you love, you stare at the studio photo, and you spend the next twenty minutes trying to mentally place it under your couch.

Most stores try to solve this by:

- Adding more photos (helps a little)
- Including dimensions (necessary, not sufficient)
- Offering free returns (expensive for the merchant, friction for the shopper)

None of those answer the actual question. **"Will this rug work in my room?"** is a question about *your* room, and a product page that can't see your room can only suggest.

## What the widget does

Sceneva drops a single line of code onto your product pages. Shoppers tap a button, upload a photo of their actual room, and within seconds they see the rug rendered in their own space. Not a generic mock-up — their floor, their lighting, their furniture.

The widget:

- **Auto-detects** your product image on most major themes (Shopify, WooCommerce, Webflow, custom)
- **Renders in seconds** via gpt-image-1.5
- **Costs you nothing per shopper** until they convert (monthly bundle pricing)

## Why now

Two things changed in the last six months:

1. **Image-to-image models got good enough** to handle interior lighting and perspective without producing uncanny results.
2. **The per-image cost dropped** below what it costs to send a sample rug. We can finally offer this at a price that makes sense for boutique retailers, not just enterprise.

## Who it's for

If you sell anything that lives in a room — rugs, wall art, large furniture, lighting — and your product photography is "honest catalog" rather than "professionally staged in every imaginable interior," you'll feel the lift.
